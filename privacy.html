⚡ atworth — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

atworth ("the app", "we") is a personal finance and life‑management app. This policy explains what data we handle and how. We built atworth to keep your data yours.

1. What we store

The information you enter — transactions, bank balances, budgets, goals, habits, journal entries, investments and loans — is stored:

  • Locally on your device (your browser/app storage). This is the primary copy.
  • Optionally in the cloud, only if you create an account or sign in with Google, so your data can sync across devices.

2. Account & sign‑in

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google solely to identify your account and sync your data. If you register with email, we store your name, email and mobile number for login and account recovery. Passwords are stored in hashed form.

3. Voice features

Voice commands use your browser/device's built‑in speech recognition. Audio is processed by your device/browser provider and is not recorded or stored by atworth. We only act on the recognized text to perform the action you asked for.

4. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal or financial data.
  • We do not share your financial data with advertisers.
  • We do not access your real bank accounts — you enter balances manually or paste statements.

5. Advertising

The website version may show Google AdSense ads to support the free service. Ads are disabled in the installed Android app. AdSense may use cookies as described in Google's advertising policy.

6. Data retention & deletion

You can export or permanently delete all your data at any time from Settings → Backup & Data and Settings → Reset All Data. Deleting clears local data; to delete cloud data, email us and we will remove your account.

7. Children

atworth is not directed to children under 13.
