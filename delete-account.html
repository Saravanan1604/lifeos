⚡ atworth — Delete Your Account & Data

Developer: Saravanan · Last updated: 2 June 2026

This page explains how to delete your atworth account and the data associated with it. You can delete your data yourself at any time, or request full account deletion by email.

Option 1 — Delete all your data in the app (instant)

  1. Open atworth
  2. Go to Settings (left menu / sidebar)
  3. Scroll to ⚠️ Danger Zone
  4. Tap “Reset All Data” and confirm

This permanently erases all data stored on your device — transactions, budgets, goals, habits, journal, investments and settings.

Option 2 — Delete your cloud account (email request)

If you created an account (email or Google sign-in) and used cloud sync, email us to delete your account and all cloud-stored data:

📧 saro8553@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my atworth account.” We will delete your account and associated cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

  • Deleted: your name, email, phone number, and all financial, health, habit, goal and journal data — both on-device and in the cloud.
  • Kept: nothing personal is retained after deletion. Anonymous, non-identifying logs (if any) may persist briefly for security/abuse prevention and are purged on a rolling basis.
