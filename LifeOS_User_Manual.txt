# LifeOS - User Manual

Welcome to **LifeOS**, your all-in-one personal operating system! This simple guide will help you understand how to use the app to manage your money, habits, health, and goals.

---

## 1. Getting Started
* **Create an Account:** Open the app and click "Create Account". Enter your name, email, and password. 
* **Cloud Sync:** Your data is automatically saved to the cloud. You can log in from your phone, tablet, or computer, and all your data will be there!

## 2. The Dashboard (Home)
When you log in, you will see your main Dashboard.
* **Level & XP:** You earn XP (Experience Points) by completing habits and adding journal entries. Keep using the app to Level Up!
* **Quick Stats:** See a quick summary of your total bank balance, today's spending, and your active habits.

## 3. Finance & Money
Tap **"Finance"** on the menu to manage your money.
* **Bank Accounts:** Tap "+ Add Account" to add your bank accounts (e.g., Savings, Current) and set your current balance.
* **Credit Cards:** Add your credit cards to track how much you owe. The app shows a color-coded bar (Green/Red) to warn you if you are using too much credit.
* **Transactions:** Click "+ New Transaction" to record your Income (money coming in) or Expenses (money going out). You can categorize them (like Food, Shopping, Salary).
* **Budgets:** Want to save money? Set a monthly budget limit for different categories. The app will show a progress bar and warn you if you go over budget!

## 4. Habits & Goals
Tap **"Habits"** to build better daily routines.
* Add daily habits (like "Drink Water", "Exercise", or "Read").
* Tap the checkbox next to a habit every day to complete it and earn XP.
* Set long-term goals and mark them as complete when you achieve them.

## 5. Health & Wellbeing
Tap **"Health"** to track your physical fitness.
* Keep a log of your weight, sleep hours, or medical history so you never lose track of your health data.

## 6. Growth (Emotion Journal)
Tap **"Growth"** to access your personal journal.
* **Daily Check-in:** Choose your mood for the day on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Tags:** Select emotion tags (Happy, Stressed, Calm, etc.).
* **Notes:** Write down your thoughts or what happened that day.
* **Past Entries:** You can click the date picker at the top to write or edit entries for past dates!

## 7. AI Assistant & Tools
* **Ask AI:** Use the built-in AI chat to ask questions about finance, health, or get life advice.
* **Calculator:** Use the built-in calculator for quick math without leaving the app.

---
**📱 Tip for Mobile Users:** 
You can install LifeOS directly on your phone! Open the app URL in Google Chrome on your Android, tap the 3-dot menu, and select **"Add to Home Screen"**. It will install just like an app from the Play Store!
